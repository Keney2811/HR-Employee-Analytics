# HR Employee Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee data to understand workforce characteristics and identify patterns associated with employee attrition.

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel and involved data cleaning, exploratory analysis, PivotTables, KPI calculations, and dashboard creation.

## Business Questions

- What percentage of employees have left the company?
- Which employee characteristics and workplace factors are associated with higher or lower attrition?
- How does attrition vary across departments, job roles, age groups, income levels, overtime status, job satisfaction, and years of experience?
- Which employee groups show higher attrition rates, and what areas could HR investigate to improve employee retention?

## Dataset

The dataset contains employee information including:

- Employee ID
- Age
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Years at Company
- Years with Current Manager
- Overtime
- Attrition
- Education
- Marital Status
- Business Travel
- Job Level
- Total Working Years

## Data Cleaning

The dataset was prepared for analysis by:

- Checking for duplicate employee records
- Checking for missing values
- Reviewing categorical variables for consistency
- Standardizing relevant categorical fields
- Creating age groups
- Creating salary/income groups
- Preparing the dataset for PivotTable analysis

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks key HR metrics including:

- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Job Satisfaction

## Analysis

The analysis examined employee attrition across several workforce characteristics, including:

- Department
- Job Role
- Age Group
- Monthly Income
- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Years of Experience
- Years with Current Manager

## Key Findings

- The overall employee attrition rate was **16.09%**.
- The majority of employees in the dataset remained with the company, while a smaller proportion left.
- Average monthly income differed across departments.
- Average job satisfaction varied across departments and age groups.
- Employee income also varied substantially across job roles.
- Attrition patterns were further examined across employee characteristics and workplace factors to identify areas requiring additional HR investigation.
